Services

Treatment Focused on How You Feel and Move

Every session is adapted to your needs. Treatment can focus on:

Therapeutic Massage

Targeted hands-on work to ease muscular tension and support everyday movement.

Deep Tissue Work

Firmer pressure for stubborn tension in the neck, shoulders, back, and hips.

Relaxation Massage

A slower, calming treatment to help you unwind and reset.

Sports & Activity Recovery

Treatment to help you recover from training, recreation, or an active weekend.

Posture & Desk Tension

Focused work for stiffness from long hours at a desk or behind the wheel.

Low Back & Hip Tightness

Care for the lower back, glutes, and hips to help restore comfortable movement.

Stress & Headache Relief

Gentle treatment for tension held in the head, neck, and shoulders.

Full-Body Maintenance

Regular treatment to keep you feeling balanced and moving well.
