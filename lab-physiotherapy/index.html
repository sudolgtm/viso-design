Your Physiotherapist

Meet Ben White

Ben bridges the gap between clinical rehab and elite athletic performance. Holding a Masters of Physiotherapy (Leeds Beckett) and a Bachelor of Human Kinetics (UBCO), he combines advanced clinical skills with heavy strength and conditioning principles.

As a former Okanagan Sun football and UBCO Rugby 7s player, Ben understands high-level sport and what it takes to recover. He specializes in sports performance, post-surgical rehab, and active longevity, using data-driven force testing, an exercise focus, advanced modalities, and skilled manual therapy.
